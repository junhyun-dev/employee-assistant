The Handbook repository distributes its software and associated documentation under the MIT license included in `third_party/gitlab-handbook-LICENSE.txt`. The policy file and its containing directories did not identify a different license when checked on 2026-09-23. This project retains the full upstream notice for the bundled excerpts and their policy-derived summaries.

The sample was extracted from the rendered page on 2026-09-23. Its six entries preserve selected text, not complete sections or the entire policy. Section IDs, grouping, extraction metadata, Korean explanations and rule-composed responses were added by this project. The stored `source_line_range` values refer to the original rendered-page extraction, not stable line numbers in the Markdown source. The excerpt sentences were also checked against the pinned source above with Markdown links and formatting removed.

GitLab's policy is used as an external demonstration dataset. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by GitLab, and its outputs do not establish any employee's eligibility, remaining allowance or approval. Consult the current source and the relevant organization for actual decisions.
